Our story

Built on local relationships.

Vizion Insurance Company, Ltd. was incorporated in 2017 and began its non-life insurance business in 2020.

We started by offering automobile insurance, workers' compensation and construction bonds, and quickly established a strong presence in the CNMI by providing general casualty, property and surety insurance. Today, automobile insurance is our largest line of business.

Our team works from our office on Beach Road in San Jose, where customers can sit down with the people who look after their policies.
