Insurance & bonds

Insurance solutions for all.

Vizion provides general casualty, property and surety insurance to individuals, families and businesses in the CNMI.

Every policy is different. The descriptions on this page are general. Our underwriters will explain the coverage, terms and options that apply to you when you meet with them.

Our largest line of business

Automobile insurance

Helps protect you from the financial cost of accidents and other incidents involving your vehicle, whether you drive for yourself or for your business.

Suited to

  • Individuals and families with a personal vehicle
  • Businesses with company cars, vans or trucks

For employers

Workers' compensation

Helps employers meet the cost of work-related injuries and illnesses, so employees can get care and support while they recover.

Suited to

  • Businesses and organizations with employees on payroll
  • Contractors with staff working on site

Property

Fire & builder's risk

Fire insurance protects buildings and their contents against loss or damage caused by fire. Builder's risk protects a structure, and the materials for it, while it is under construction.

Suited to

  • Owners of homes and commercial buildings
  • Contractors and project owners with construction under way

For businesses

Liability insurance

Helps protect your business if it is held legally responsible for injury to other people or damage to their property.

Suited to

  • Businesses that serve customers or the public
  • Contractors and service providers working at clients' sites

Property

Burglary insurance

Helps cover the loss of insured property, and damage to your premises, resulting from a forcible break-in.

Suited to

  • Businesses with stock or equipment on site
  • Owners of property they want to protect against theft

Surety

Bonds

A bond is a guarantee that an obligation will be met. Bonds give project owners confidence in a contractor, and help contractors win work that requires one.

Suited to

  • Contractors bidding for public or private projects
  • Businesses required by contract to post a bond
The four types of bond

Surety bonds

Bonds for contractors and project owners.

  • Bid bond

    Guarantees that, if your bid is accepted, you will sign the contract and provide any required performance and payment bonds.

  • Performance bond

    Guarantees to the project owner that the work will be completed according to the terms of the contract.

  • Payment bond

    Guarantees that subcontractors, workers and suppliers will be paid for the labor and materials they provide.

  • Surety bond

    Other bonds that guarantee one party's obligation to another, arranged to suit what your contract requires.
