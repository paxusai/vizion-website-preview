Customer service

Personal service, every step of the way.

Insurance is personal, so we handle it personally. Our team at the Beach Road office looks after every stage of your policy, from the first conversation to renewals, payments and claims.

Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM

How we can help

Talk to the people who know your policy.

Whatever you need, our team will take care of it with you, in person or over the phone.

  • New policies & quotations

    Tell our underwriters what you'd like to protect. They'll explain your options and prepare a quotation for you.

  • Renewals

    Speak with us before your policy expires. We'll review any changes with you and help keep your cover in place.

  • Payments & billing

    Premiums can be paid at our office counter, and our billing team can help with statements of account.

  • Policy changes

    New vehicle, new address or a change in your business? Let us know and we'll explain how it affects your cover.

  • Documents & forms

    Our customer care team can provide copies of your policy documents and the right forms for your situation.

  • Claims assistance

    If something goes wrong, contact us as soon as you can. Our claims team will guide you through what's needed.

Claims assistance

We're here when something goes wrong.

Every claim is handled personally by our claims team, who will explain what's needed and keep you informed along the way.

Each claim is reviewed against the terms, conditions and exclusions of your policy.

In an emergency, call 911 first.

If anyone is hurt or in danger, contact emergency services before anything else.

  1. Make sure everyone is safe

    People come first. Take care of anyone who is hurt and move to a safe place if you can.

  2. Contact Vizion as soon as you can

    Call or visit our office and let us know what happened.

  3. We'll guide you from there

    Our claims team will tell you exactly what they need and what happens next.
