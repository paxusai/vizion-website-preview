Company profile

A Saipan insurer, built on trust.

Vizion Insurance Company, Ltd. is a non-life insurance company based in Saipan, Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.

Since its official launch in 2020, Vizion has rapidly grown to become a trusted partner for individuals, businesses and clients seeking reliable insurance solutions. We provide general casualty, property and surety insurance, from automobile and workers' compensation cover to construction bonds.

In 2025, Vizion strengthened its stability and capacity by securing reinsurance support through Everest Re and China Re, enhancing our ability to manage risk and protect our growing portfolio.
