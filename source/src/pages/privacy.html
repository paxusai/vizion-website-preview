Privacy

Privacy notice.

How this website handles your information. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Vizion Insurance Company, Ltd., 2360 Beach Road, San Jose, Saipan, CNMI. You can reach us about privacy using the details on our contact page.

What this website collects

This website is for information only. It has no forms, user accounts or online services, does not set its own cookies, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. It does not collect personal information.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The fonts on this site are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your device's IP address and browser details in order to deliver them.
  • Google Maps. The map on our contact page loads only if you press "Show interactive map". Google's privacy policy then applies to the map. Direction links open Google Maps in a new tab.
  • Facebook. Links to our Facebook page take you to Facebook, where Facebook's privacy policy applies.

When you contact us

If you call, email or visit us, we use the information you share to respond to you and to provide our insurance services. Please don't send ID, bank or card numbers by email. Our team will tell you how to provide sensitive information safely.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the website changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
